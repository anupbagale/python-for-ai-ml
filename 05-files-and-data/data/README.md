# Data

## penguins.csv

Measurements of 344 penguins of three species (Adelie, Chinstrap and Gentoo), observed on three islands in the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica, between 2007 and 2009.

| column | description |
|---|---|
| `species` | Adelie, Chinstrap or Gentoo |
| `island` | Biscoe, Dream or Torgersen |
| `bill_length_mm` | bill (culmen) length in millimetres |
| `bill_depth_mm` | bill (culmen) depth in millimetres |
| `flipper_length_mm` | flipper length in millimetres |
| `body_mass_g` | body mass in grams |
| `sex` | male or female |
| `year` | year of observation |

Missing values are written as `NA`.

**Source.** The data was collected and made available by Dr. Kristen Gorman and the Palmer Station Long Term Ecological Research (LTER) Program, part of the US Long Term Ecological Research Network. It was packaged as the `palmerpenguins` dataset by Allison Horst, Alison Hill and Kristen Gorman: <https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/>
