# Riverside Community Library: Member Handbook

This handbook is invented for the course. Riverside Community Library is not a real library, so no language model can know these details from its training data.

## Opening hours

The main library on Mill Street is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 19:00, and Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. The Harbour branch opens only on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, from 10:00 to 15:00.

## Joining the library

Membership is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in Riverside. To join, bring a photo ID and something showing your address, such as a utility bill or a student card. Children under 12 can join with a parent or guardian. New members receive a green library card on the spot.

## Borrowing books

Members can borrow up to 12 items at a time. Books can be kept for 3 weeks, DVDs and video games for 1 week. Children's picture books can be kept for 6 weeks.

## Renewing loans

Loans can be renewed up to 3 times, online through the member portal, by phone on 0123 456 789, or at any desk. An item cannot be renewed if another member has reserved it.

## Late returns

Riverside stopped charging late fees for books in 2023. Items more than 8 weeks overdue are treated as lost, and the member is charged the replacement cost. DVDs and video games returned late are charged 50 pence per day, up to a maximum of 5 pounds per item.

## Lost or damaged items

If you lose or damage an item, tell a librarian as soon as possible. You pay the replacement cost, and the charge is refunded if you find and return the item within 3 months.

## Lost library cards

A lost card can be replaced at any desk for 2 pounds. Bring a photo ID. The first replacement for children under 16 is free. Report a lost card quickly, because anyone holding it can borrow items in your name.

## Reservations

You can reserve any item in the catalogue for free. When it arrives, we send an email or text message and keep it for you at the desk for 7 days.

## Computers and printing

Members can use the public computers for 2 hours a day without charge; book a computer at the desk or online. Printing costs 10 pence per page in black and white and 40 pence per page in colour. Free Wi-Fi is available to everyone, members or not, with the network name RiversideLibrary.

## Study rooms

The Mill Street library has four study rooms for groups of 2 to 6 people. Rooms can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance, for a maximum of 3 hours a day. Food is not allowed in the study rooms, but drinks in closed containers are fine.

## Events

The library runs a free storytime for children under 5 every Wednesday at 10:30, a coding club for ages 10 to 15 on Thursdays at 16:00, and a monthly book group on the first Monday of the month at 18:30. Events are free but places at the coding club must be booked in advance.

## Accessibility

Both buildings have step-free access and an accessible toilet. Large-print books and audiobooks are available, and the Mill Street library has a reading magnifier and a hearing loop at the main desk. Members who cannot visit can use the free home delivery service, run by volunteers every second Friday.

## Donations

The library accepts donations of books published in the last 5 years, in good condition. We do not accept textbooks, magazines or encyclopaedias. Books we cannot use are given to the Riverside charity shop.

## Contact

Email help@riverside-library.example, phone 0123 456 789, or ask at any desk. The phone line is open during Mill Street opening hours.
